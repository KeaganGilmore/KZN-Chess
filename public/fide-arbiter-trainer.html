How to use this

A sequence that works for most candidates.

  1. 1 Read a Study topic end to end — each card is one article or regulation, summarised in arbiter language with the citation you must be able to quote.
  2. 2 Drill that single category in Practice mode until you are above 85%. Wrong answers come back more often automatically.
  3. 3 Switch to Timed mode. Arbiters rule under pressure; so does the examiner.
  4. 4 Once every category is green, run the Exam simulation cold. Anything under 80% points at a topic to re-read.

KZN Chess · FIDE Arbiter Trainer. Study notes are original summaries written for revision; they paraphrase rather than reproduce the regulations, and the article references point you to the authoritative text.

Always rule from the current text in the FIDE Handbook: the Laws of Chess in force from 1 January 2023, the Play-Off and Tie-Break Regulations (C.07) in force from 1 March 2026, the Swiss pairing rules (C.04) in force from 1 February 2026, and the Fair Play regulations. Where an event has its own regulations, those take precedence on the points they cover.
